The RCD keeps tripping, a circuit is dead, a socket is warm or a light flickers? We find the cause and fix it properly.

Report a fault

Fault finding

A tripping RCD often has a single cause: a defective appliance, moisture in an outdoor socket or a damaged cable. We measure circuit by circuit until we find it.

  • RCD or circuit breaker that keeps tripping
  • Sockets or lights that no longer work
  • Warm or damaged sockets and switches
  • Replacing old switches, sockets and fittings
Example photo

What to do before we arrive

Note which circuit tripped and what was switched on at that moment. Unplug appliances on that circuit and try switching it back on. Smell burning, or see scorch marks? Leave the circuit off and call us.
