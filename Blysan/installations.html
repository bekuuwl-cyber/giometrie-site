New build or full renovation

We plan the installation with you: where sockets, switches and lights go, which circuits you need, and how much room to keep in the distribution board for later (an EV charger, a heat pump or solar panels).

  • Plan of sockets, switches and lighting points
  • Cabling and conduits, built in or surface-mounted
  • New distribution board with the right protection per circuit
  • Single-line diagram and layout plan for the inspection

Ready for the AREI inspection

In Belgium every new installation is inspected by an accredited body before it is connected, and a home that is sold needs an inspection report as well. We prepare the installation and the documents, and are there on the day if you wish.

Has an inspection found problems? We work through the report point by point and bring the installation in line.
