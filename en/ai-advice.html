AI advice

Using AI, without the AI look.

AI look
Real look

Both photos were made with AI. The difference is in how you use AI. Slide to compare.

More and more businesses use AI, and it shows: the same glossy images, the same sentences. Customers spot in one second that something was ‘made with AI’, and then your business looks cheap and impersonal. An AI photo of a dish that looks different in real life even costs you customers.

I teach you to use AI well: as a tool that follows your style, not as a replacement for it. You save time, and the result is still recognisably yours.

01

AI is the tool, not the craftsman

A good hammer doesn't make a carpenter. The result depends on who uses it, and that can be learned.

02

Review of your material

I look at your flyers, posts and texts, and show you what gives away ‘AI’ and how to do better.

03

Tailored advice

Together we look at how you or your team use AI today. I advise on what can be improved and which tools suit your business.

04

Your own AI style guide

We capture your brand, tone and examples, so every AI tool follows your style instead of the AI style.

With respect for your privacy and your business data.

AI assistant

A chatbot that sounds like your business.

An AI assistant on your website answers customers' questions, also in the evening and at the weekend. It checks your stock, knows your prices and opening hours, and answers in your style. If it doesn't know something, it refers to you instead of making things up.

  • Answers based on your business's information
  • Tells whether something is in stock, linked to your till or webshop
  • Puts products aside or records a request
  • Also speaks French or Dutch if your customer does
